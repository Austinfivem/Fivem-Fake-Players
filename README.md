# Austin Fake Players for FiveM

Austin Fake Players is a service for FiveM server owners who want to display a simulated player count. A control panel lets you choose how many simulated players to display, set their names, and turn them on or off. The simulated count is separate from the number of people actually playing on your server.

## How to order

1. Choose **Monthly** or **Infinity** and complete checkout in the [Austin store](https://fivemaustin.mysellauth.com/).
2. Join the [Austin Discord](https://discord.gg/2ndvvVnXMf) and open a support ticket.
3. In the ticket, send your **invoice ID** and either your **FiveM join link** (`cfx.re/join/xxxxxx`) or your **server ID**.
4. Once your service is active, use the control panel to set player names, adjust the count, or switch the simulated players off.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a higher displayed count mean more people are playing?**  
The service displays simulated players. Use your actual player count when reporting community activity.

**Will it affect server performance?**  
We haven't published CPU, memory, or latency benchmarks. If performance matters for your setup, ask about testing before you order.

**Can my server be blacklisted?**  
We can't guarantee protection from platform enforcement. Check the current FiveM server rules before enabling the service.

**Where can I get help with my order?**  
Open a ticket in the [Austin Discord](https://discord.gg/2ndvvVnXMf). Share your invoice ID in the ticket so support can locate your order.
