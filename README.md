# Austin Fake Players for FiveM

Austin Fake Players lets FiveM server owners configure a simulated player count through a control panel. You can choose how many simulated players to display, customize their names, and turn them on or off. Keep simulated players separate when reporting how many people are actually online.

## How to Order

1. Choose **Monthly** or **Infinity** in the [Austin store](https://fivemaustin.mysellauth.com/) and complete checkout.
2. Join the [Austin Discord](https://discord.gg/2ndvvVnXMf) and open a support ticket.
3. In the ticket, send your **invoice ID** and either your **FiveM join link** (`cfx.re/join/xxxxxx`) or your **server ID**.
4. Once the service is active, open the control panel to set the player count, customize names, and turn simulated players on or off.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the displayed count tell me how many people are playing?**  
The displayed count can include simulated players. Use your actual player count when reporting community activity.

**Will the service affect server performance?**  
We haven't published CPU, memory, or latency benchmarks. If you need performance figures for your setup, ask about testing before you order.

**Can my server be blacklisted?**  
We can't guarantee protection from platform enforcement. Review the current FiveM server rules before enabling the service.

**Where can I get help with my order?**  
Open a ticket in the [Austin Discord](https://discord.gg/2ndvvVnXMf) and include your invoice ID so support can find your order.
